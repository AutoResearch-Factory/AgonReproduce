# Report Critique

Three levels of review for reliability reports.

## Structural Critic

Check target, scope, claim table, evidence sections, failure attribution, limitations, and replay instructions. A report fails structurally if a reader cannot tell what was audited, what was not audited, and what evidence supports each verdict.

## Evidence Critic

Look for claim/evidence mismatch, missing source_refs, over-strong verdicts, hidden failed runs, cherry-picked numbers, unsupported citations, and trace gaps. Any false-accusation risk is CRITICAL.

## Section Critic

Review one section for clarity, completeness, evidence, and consistency with other sections. Each section must answer: what claim or uncertainty does this reduce, and where can the reader verify it?
