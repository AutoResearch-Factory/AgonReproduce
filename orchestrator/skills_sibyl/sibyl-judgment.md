# Audit Judgment

## Backwards Reasoning

When results contradict expectations, identify which assumption failed: claim interpretation, artifact availability, protocol, metric, implementation, environment, or target claim. Revise the hypothesis and design the smallest check that distinguishes alternatives.

## Engineering Realism

Prefer existing code/artifacts with clear provenance over complex custom machinery. Feasibility depends on infrastructure, implementation simplicity, data access, compute budget, and trace quality.

If a route exceeds budget, narrow the verdict or mark it out of budget; do not silently lower the success standard.
