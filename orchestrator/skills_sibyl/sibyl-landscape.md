# Reliability Landscape

## Evidence Classification

- `direct_support`: prior work reproduced or independently supports the same claim.
- `partial_support`: supports a narrower condition or proxy.
- `contradiction`: credible evidence conflicts with the claim.
- `artifact_note`: code/data/protocol availability or failure.
- `critique`: methodological or statistical concern.
- `mere_mention`: cites or discusses target without evidence.

## Survey Principles

Speed matters, but classification matters more than count. Record only sources that affect claim confidence, route feasibility, or report limitations.

## Output

For each source: citation/URL, relation type, affected claim_id, evidence strength, limitation, and implication for STATE/INVES planning.
