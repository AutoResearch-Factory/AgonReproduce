# Verification Check Design

Three lenses for generating audit checks.

## Mechanism Lens

Ask which mechanism-level claim in the target paper can be checked directly, and what evidence would distinguish support from alternative explanations.

## Transfer Lens

Borrow verification methods from adjacent domains: metadata checks, artifact smoke tests, protocol replay, statistical consistency checks, citation-context audits.

## Cross-Domain Evidence Lens

Look outside the paper's immediate field for evidence sources: benchmark leaderboards, issue trackers, replications, corrections, regulatory records, dataset cards, model cards, or community critiques.

Every check must end as a claim-bound evidence question, not a new research direction.
